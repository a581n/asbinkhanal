About Me

I am a Biotechnology graduate from Kathmandu University with a strong foundation in research, leadership, and organisational development. My interests lie in biotechnology research, molecular docking, antimicrobial studies, and project-driven innovation. I enjoy working in interdisciplinary environments where science, leadership, and problem-solving intersect to create real-world impact.
